ℹ️ What is Solar Fishing?

Solar Fishing is a lightweight Roblox Fisch fishing macro built for simplicity and long-term stability, designed to provide reliable, hands-free fishing with no setup.

🎯 Core Features Breakdown

Overview
Your central hub for managing fishing profiles, monitoring macro status, and accessing general settings.
Cast
Configure automated casting, including casting behavior, timing, and related detection settings.
Shake
Customize shake minigame detection and automation to handle different shake interfaces and configurations.
Fish
Configure fish detection and automated fishing controls, including color detection, predictive control, and fishing behavior.
Utilities
Access additional fishing-related automation tools designed to simplify gameplay and reduce repetitive tasks.

📥 Installation & Setup Guide

🚀 Downloading and using Solar Fishing Lite is straightforward.

Step 1: Download the Solar Fishing Lite .ahk script from the Downloads panel on the right.

Step 2: Double-click the .ahk file to launch Solar Fishing Lite.

Step 3: Open Roblox Fisch and click Detect Roblox in Solar Fishing Lite. Select your rod and configure your preferred settings.

That's it! Solar Fishing Lite will work perfectly.
